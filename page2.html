About this graph

This is the public automatic plot from the Széchenyi István Geophysical Observatory near Nagycenk, Hungary (IAGA code NCK). It has been monitoring Schumann resonances with a ball antenna since the early 1990s.

The style matches the amplitude charts many people follow on sites such as Disclosure News (which also draws from Nagycenk data). The spectrograms show energy across 0–40 Hz; the bright horizontal bands are the resonance modes. The lower plots track how strong each of the first three modes is and where their peak frequencies sit throughout the day.

Source: © Széchenyi István Geophysical Observatory. Data is scientific observation, not a global “power” score.
